# Logos

These logos are the trademarks of their owners. nori uses them only to identify the apps it opens
files from and the services it works with, next to their names; it isn't endorsed by or affiliated
with them.

Each file was downloaded on 2026-10-07 (the ones carried over from kimchi on 2026-10-05 and
2026-10-06), then only rasterised and resized to 128 × 128 px: SVG files were rendered with resvg
(`@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6), transparent margins trimmed and the content centred with ImageMagick,
transparency kept. Nothing was redrawn. Simple Icons artwork is CC0 and is drawn in one colour, so
it gets the brand colour from Simple Icons' own data file (field `hex`). A mark that is black, or
nearly, gets a `-dark` copy in white (or with its colours inverted) for the dark theme.
